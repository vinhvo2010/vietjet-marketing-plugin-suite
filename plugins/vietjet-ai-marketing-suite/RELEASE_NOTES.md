# Release notes — v5.0.0

Release date: 2026-09-07

## Outcome

V5 upgrades the suite from 12 functional specialists plus workflow prompts to a Level-2 coordinated Vietjet Group Marketing AI Team with 18 canonical roles, 24 discoverable skills, deterministic team routing, dependency waves, typed work objects, independent review and explicit human gates.

## Added

- VJ-GMOM strategic operating model and research/claim-source ledger.
- Group Brand & Portfolio, Customer Journey & Experience, Marketing Science & Experimentation, Content & Organic Discovery, MarTech & AI Operations and Market Pod Lead.
- Mission routing commands: `team-plan` and `team-prompts`.
- Seven JSON Schemas for mission, evidence, decision, experiment, release, learning and handoff.
- Crisis override, narrow-task routing and route-corridor team plans.
- Market-scope separation for operating entity, point of sale, source market and route corridor.
- Public-source baselines for Vietnam, Thailand, Australia, India and Mainland China.
- Airline-commerce, customer-data-platform and marketing-measurement connector blueprints.
- Safe explicit-target installer with validation-before-copy.

## Changed

- North Star now balances incremental contribution, customer value, future demand and operating trust.
- Campaign lifecycle now follows `SENSE → CHOOSE → DESIGN OFFER → CREATE → ACTIVATE → SERVICE → MEASURE → LEARN`.
- Vietnam privacy baseline updated from the v4 Decree-only reference to Law 91/2025/QH15 plus current implementing-rule verification.
- Handoff is now `STATUS / DECISION / EVIDENCE / OUTPUT / APPROVALS / NEXT`.
- Outputs are modular work objects rather than a mandatory 12-document ceremony.

## Validation evidence

- Dependency-free suite validator: passed.
- Runtime registry, route-team, crisis-override and narrow-routing tests: passed.
- Official plugin validator: passed.
- Official skill validator: 24/24 passed.
- Strict output checker for rules, agents, skills, docs and research: zero warnings/blocks.
- Explicit-target clean install followed by installed `doctor`: passed.

## Known production gates

Level 3/4 is intentionally not claimed. Production rollout still requires named approvers, real aggregate data contracts, connector identities/scopes, platform-enforced controls, experiment execution, publication/spend controls, observability, rollback and kill-switch rehearsal.
