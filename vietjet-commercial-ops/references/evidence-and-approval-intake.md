# Evidence and Approval Intake

Use this intake before producing any commercial, promotional, CRM, route, governance, PMO, or executive output. Do not treat completion of this template as approval.

## Intake Template

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Output ID | Use [Output ID Convention](output-id-convention.md), or `PENDING` until assigned |
| Request owner | Named human owner or confirmed role |
| Business unit | Business unit and market scope |
| Business objective | Decision, outcome, or question this output must support |
| Requested skill | Skill expected to produce the output |
| Intended usage channel | Internal brief, leadership review, CRM, social, web, app, OTA, PR, or other |
| Intended audience | Internal role/team or external audience |
| Time sensitivity | Decision deadline, publish date, booking/travel window, or `NOT PROVIDED` |
| Risk level | `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, or `CRITICAL`, with reason |
| Approval owner | Confirmed human approver or `POLICY TO CONFIRM` |
| Approval status | `NOT REQUESTED`, `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS`, or `REJECTED` |
| Final decision | `PENDING`, `PROCEED`, `PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS`, `DEFER`, or `STOP` |

## Completion Gate

- [ ] Request owner, objective, usage channel, and time sensitivity recorded
- [ ] Facts separated from assumptions and missing data
- [ ] Evidence references and source owners recorded
- [ ] Data-boundary and connector restrictions checked
- [ ] Risk level recorded with reason
- [ ] Approval owner and status recorded
- [ ] Final decision recorded by a human

If a required field is unavailable, write `DATA UNAVAILABLE` or `POLICY TO CONFIRM`; never infer it.
