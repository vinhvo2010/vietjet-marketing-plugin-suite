# Vietjet Commercial Ops v0.1.0 Controlled Internal Pilot

**Pilot date:** June 8, 2026  
**Method:** Three governed simulations using only facts supplied in the current thread and existing pilot brief. No connectors, external sources, market data, revenue data, inventory data, customer-level data, or approval policy were accessed.  
**Status:** DRAFT - INTERNAL REVIEW REQUIRED

## Pilot Governance

- All outputs are working drafts, not authorization to publish, launch, send, activate, or execute.
- Statements supplied by the user are classified as `BRIEF FACT`; they still require confirmation from the designated data or operational owner before external use.
- Assumptions are labeled and are not presented as facts.
- Missing evidence is labeled `DATA UNAVAILABLE`.
- Approval roles and thresholds remain `POLICY TO CONFIRM`.
- No customer-level PII or PNR data is used.

## Pilot Readiness Recommendation

### Decision

**v0.1.0 is ready for controlled internal team use.**

The pilot demonstrates that the plugin can turn incomplete commercial briefs into useful working drafts while consistently:

- separating brief facts, hypotheses, and unavailable data;
- refusing to invent market, revenue, inventory, approval, or route-performance information;
- producing actionable data requests and human decision gates;
- protecting CRM data boundaries and transparent upsell behavior;
- treating outputs as drafts rather than operational authorization.

### Internal-Use Conditions

- Use with pasted or authorized data only.
- Keep outputs inside the human review and approval workflow.
- Require designated data owners to verify material facts and numbers.
- Do not use pilot outputs to publish, launch, activate, or approve without confirmed policy and evidence.
- Record user, inputs, sources, output, and resulting human decision for pilot audit.

## Critical or High-Value Refinements for v0.1.1

1. **Add a standard evidence-and-approval intake template.**  
   A reusable front page should capture business unit, source owner, source date, sensitivity, approval status, and intended use before any skill run. This would reduce repeated clarification and strengthen auditability.

2. **Add deterministic post-campaign and CRM measurement worksheets.**  
   Provide validated templates or scripts for channel parity, promo recap metrics, randomized holdout checks, and gross-versus-incremental calculations. This would reduce calculation and interpretation risk when authorized aggregate data becomes available.

3. **Add a pilot audit-log schema and output ID convention.**  
   Standardize who ran each skill, supplied sources, assumptions, approvals required, version, and final human decision. This is the highest-value operational control before broader internal adoption.

No other feature work is required before controlled internal team use.
