# Campaign Business Case Standard

## Scenario Boundary

- Assumption-only numbers must not be shown as a validated or approved business case.
- Scenario figures must be labeled `ASSUMPTION_ONLY` or `CALCULATION_FROM_ASSUMPTIONS`.
- Clipboard tools must not copy unverified numbers as an approved summary.

## Revenue Boundary

- Gross revenue is not incremental revenue.
- Incremental revenue requires an evidence-backed counterfactual, such as a valid holdout/control design.
- ROI requires verified cost and verified revenue data.
- Unsupported budget, booking, ABV, revenue, inventory, fare, and market figures remain blocked from external use.
