# Lightweight Planning Scenario

## Arguments

The objective, supplied inputs, evidence, and requested decision are in `$ARGUMENTS`.

## Workflow

1. Read `skills/revenue-governance/SKILL.md`,
   `references/campaign-business-case-standard.md`, and
   `references/operating-governance.md`.
2. Use `skills/revenue-pmo/SKILL.md` only when the user explicitly requests an execution backlog;
   do not create a 12-week plan by default.
3. Separate verified inputs, assumptions, calculations from assumptions, and missing validation
   data.
4. Produce formulas and scenario ranges only when inputs support them. Label every scenario number
   as an assumption, not a forecast or approved target.
5. Keep gross revenue separate from incremental revenue. Treat incrementality as not measurable
   without a valid control or comparison.
6. Do not claim ROI without verified cost and verified revenue evidence.

## Required Output

- Decision question
- Available facts and missing inputs
- Assumption register
- Lightweight planning-scenario table
- Gross versus incremental measurement boundary
- Validation backlog and human decision needs

End with the operating-governance end-of-run block. Do not present the result as a validated
business case.
