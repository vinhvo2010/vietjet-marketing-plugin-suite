# Skill Index

| Skill | When to use | Required inputs | Main output | Key risks controlled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `brand-voice` | Rewriting campaign, CRM, PR, executive, or internal copy | Source text, channel, audience, constraints | Diagnosed and rewritten variants with review notes | Changed facts, unsupported claims, misleading free/universal language, off-register copy |
| `campaign-factory` | Building an end-to-end seasonal, route, promo, or brand campaign | Objective, routes/products, audience, dates, offer, budget tier, constraints, yield status | Campaign insight, messaging, requested-channel execution pack, approvals, dashboard spec | Unverified timely claims, missing yield approval, unsupported competitor references, omitted channel constraints |
| `crm-personalization` | Designing lifecycle journeys, ancillary upsells, or CRM measurement | Aggregate segment/journey context, goal, channels, offer logic, available performance data | Segment definition, journey map, copy variants, suppressions, experiment plan | PII exposure, dark patterns, missing suppressions, gross-versus-incremental confusion, selection bias |
| `executive-reporter` | Preparing leadership one-pagers, board-style updates, or decision briefs | Verified metrics/source material, context, audience, decision requested | Numbers-first brief with options, trade-offs, decisions, and next steps | Invented metrics, unsourced claims, hidden assumptions, unclear decisions |
| `promo-qa` | Reviewing a promotion before approval or launch | Offer, routes/dates, channels, audience/volume, yield floor, constraints | Six-dimension risk review, channel-parity matrix, fixes, go/no-go recommendation | Yield leakage, unclear 0Đ/free terms, unverified inventory/universal claims, cross-channel inconsistency |
| `revenue-governance` | Drafting or reviewing commercial decision controls | Proposal or governance gap, route/product context, approved policy if available | Authority matrix, checklist, risk review, memo, or audit schema | Invented authority policy, missing approvals, unaudited decisions, unconfirmed yield floor |
| `revenue-pmo` | Operationalizing a commercial strategy or initiative | Initiative, business unit, sponsor role, timeline, success definition, known dependencies | 12-week backlog, RACI, KPI tree, risk register, cadence | Invented targets/current state, unowned work, hidden dependencies, unrealistic timelines |
| `route-marketing` | Planning a route launch, optimization, seasonal push, or competitive response | Route, route/launch type, objective, available performance and market evidence | Route fact sheet, evidence ledger, audience map, strategy, targets, action plan | Invented schedules/demand/competitor data, unsupported route claims, unverified targets |
