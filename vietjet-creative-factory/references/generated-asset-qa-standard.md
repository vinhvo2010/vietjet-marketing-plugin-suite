# Generated Asset QA Standard

Generated creative remains `DRAFT_ONLY` and `HUMAN_QA_REQUIRED` until deterministic preflight and human review evidence are logged.

Reject or block release when:

- actual MIME type does not match the extension;
- dimensions or aspect ratio do not match the intended use;
- generated text, generated logos, fake UI/signage, or unapproved/off-policy objects appear;
- a promised video deliverable is missing;
- an asset lacks manifest evidence or human QA evidence.

Only authorized humans may record human QA completion or release approval.
