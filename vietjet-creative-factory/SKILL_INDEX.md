# Skill Index

| Skill | When to use | Required inputs | Main output | Key creative risks controlled | Commercial handoff needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `image-prompt-factory` | Creating campaign images, key visuals, social assets, or adaptations | Objective, audience, channel/ratio, supplied facts, approved references, text/logo policy | QA-ready image prompt package with negative prompt and manual-design actions | Invented claims, identity drift, malformed text/logos, missing safe zones | Yes, when route/date/fare/offer/terms are unapproved |
| `video-prompt-factory` | Creating video prompts, shot plans, motion assets, or text-free footage | Objective, duration/ratio/platform, supplied facts, locks, text/subtitle/logo rules | Governed global direction, shot plan, continuity anchors, prohibitions | Accidental text/subtitles/logos, continuity drift, invented operations/claims | Yes, when commercial claims are requested |
| `carousel-series` | Building multi-slide campaigns or consistent asset families | Objective, panel count/channel, supplied facts, visual locks, approved copy/placeholders | Series system, panel plan, global prompt, manual copy and QA plan | Panel drift, invented copy/prices, inconsistent identity, unsafe text | Yes, when facts, CTA, fares, or terms are unresolved |
| `visual-qa` | Reviewing creative assets or prompt packages before human review | Asset/prompt, channel/format, supplied facts, approved lock references | PASS/FAIL/NOT VERIFIABLE review with blocking fixes | Unsupported claims, lock failures, artifacts, text/logo distortion, channel mismatch | Yes, for unresolved commercial claims |
| `aircraft-lock` | An aircraft model/livery must remain accurate and consistent | Aircraft model, approved livery reference/specification, camera/side, registration rule | Reusable aircraft lock prompt, prohibitions, QA checks | Wrong model/livery, mirrored markings, invented registration, proportion drift | No, unless tied to route/date/schedule/operational claims |
| `mascot-lock` | An approved mascot must remain consistent across assets | Approved mascot reference/specification, allowed variations, prohibited changes | Fixed-trait lock, allowed variations, reusable prompt, QA checks | Redesign, generic mascot transformation, wrong colors/face/outfit | No |
| `face-identity-lock` | An authorized person's visual identity must be preserved | Authorized reference, consent/usage scope, required traits, allowed/prohibited changes | Authorization-aware face lock and QA checks | Face drift, identity substitution, public-figure implication, impersonation, voice cloning | No |
| `typography-and-text-safety` | Planning or reviewing exact copy, subtitles, logos, disclaimers, or text-free generation | Exact approved copy/placeholders, language/channel/dimensions, font/logo references, prohibition rules | Typography/text plan, manual-design recommendation, safe zones, QA checks | Malformed text, altered logo, missing disclaimer, violated no-text/no-subtitle/no-logo rules | Yes, when copy contains unapproved commercial facts |
