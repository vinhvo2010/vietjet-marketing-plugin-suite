# Governed Route Launch

## Arguments

The route brief and authorized evidence references are in `$ARGUMENTS`.

## Workflow

1. Read `skills/route-marketing/SKILL.md`, `skills/campaign-factory/SKILL.md`, and
   `references/operating-governance.md`.
2. Create a route-launch fact sheet. Treat route, first-flight date, frequency, schedule, aircraft,
   operating carrier, and sales-open date as facts to verify unless supported by authorized
   evidence.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, audience hypotheses, and missing data.
4. Build route positioning, strategic narrative, target-audience hypotheses, messaging hierarchy,
   channel plan, and first actions.
5. Do not invent fares, frequency, market demand, competitor claims, performance, revenue, targets,
   or approval thresholds.
6. List evidence and human decisions required before external use or execution.

## Required Output

- Route-launch fact sheet and evidence ledger
- Route positioning and strategic narrative
- Audience hypotheses
- Key messages and channel plan
- Missing data and governance flags
- Human approval needs

End with the operating-governance end-of-run block. The output remains a draft.
