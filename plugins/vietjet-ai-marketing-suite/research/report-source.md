# Research source report — Vietjet Group Marketing Operating Model v5

Status: canonical research record  
Research date: 2026-09-07  
Scope: strategy, marketing science, airline retailing, AI governance and market-level operating constraints.  
Important: source material informs the design; it does not grant authority and is not a certification by Harvard, YC, IATA, NIST or any regulator.

## Research question

What operating model can turn the Vietjet suite from a governed prompt library into a coordinated, evidence-backed Group Marketing AI Team across Vietnam, Thailand and source markets/route corridors including Australia, India and China?

## Method

- Prefer primary or institution-owned sources.
- Separate observed source evidence from design inference.
- Treat law, routes, fares, schedules, platforms and market behavior as expiring information.
- Use current package inspection to distinguish implemented capability from desired maturity.
- Retain evidence-to-source traceability in `claim-source-ledger.md`.

## Findings

### Strategy and customer value

- HBS frames a digital marketing plan around goals, audience, value proposition and metrics, and uses customer journey stages plus 3C positioning. Design implication: every mission needs a decision, target/job, promise and measurement contract.
- HBS value-based strategy uses willingness to pay, price, cost and willingness to sell as a value-creation system. Design implication: Marketing should optimize incremental contribution and customer value, not media ROAS in isolation.
- HBR's Jobs to Be Done framing focuses on the progress customers seek. Design implication: market pods should organize insight around travel jobs/category entry points as well as demographics.
- Kaplan's balanced-scorecard work links customer outcomes to customer profitability and cost-to-serve. Design implication: loyalty and satisfaction are insufficient without economics.
- Porter/HBS activity-system reasoning implies that defensible strategy is a coherent set of reinforcing activities. Design implication: `SHOP → OFFER → ORDER → DELIVER → SERVICE → LEARN` should be one system.

### Marketing science

- IPA's Binet/Field research supports balancing short- and long-term effects, but allocations are context dependent. Design implication: use a dual demand engine without hard-coding 60:40.
- Ehrenberg-Bass Category Entry Points research connects brand growth to mental availability in buying situations. Design implication: future-demand work needs broad reach and CEP measurement, not engagement alone.
- Google's Meridian documentation describes aggregate, privacy-aware MMM and calibration with experiments, while its causal methodology notes observational assumptions. Design implication: triangulate MMM, experiments and attribution; never present modeled causality as automatic truth.

### Airline retailing

- IATA's NDC and Modern Airline Retailing materials connect relevant offers, dynamic bundling/pricing, distribution, payment, fulfillment and customer experience. Design implication: Marketing, RM, Distribution, Payment, Ops and CX need shared work objects and dependencies.
- IATA's Dynamic Offers material treats context-aware offer creation as a combination of continuous pricing and dynamic bundling. Design implication: Marketing communicates value but does not own fare/inventory decisions.
- Vietjet's current official promotional pages demonstrate different point-of-sale price presentations and payment methods across Australia, India and China. Design implication: country is not a sufficient scope field; point of sale and route corridor must be explicit.

### AI governance and team design

- NIST AI RMF organizes lifecycle risk work as Govern, Map, Measure and Manage, with explicit human roles, inventories and continuous governance.
- NIST's GenAI profile adds pre-deployment testing, provenance, incident handling and human oversight.
- gstack is an open-source, opinionated software delivery system whose value comes from connected specialist stages (`Think → Plan → Build → Review → Test → Ship → Reflect`), independent review and artifacts passed downstream. Design implication: adapt the operating pattern, not its software roles literally.

### Market operating constraints

- Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15 took effect on 2026-01-01; the audited v4 package's exclusive reference to Decree 13/2023 was stale and was replaced in v5 with current-law verification.
- Australia's OAIC states that APP 7 regulates direct marketing and that email/SMS are also subject to the Spam Act; easy opt-out and lawful expectations/consent matter.
- India's 2025 DPDP Rules use phased commencement. Design implication: the market profile must mark effective phase and require local counsel confirmation.
- China's PIPL and advertising law create privacy and truthfulness constraints; English translations are not a substitute for local-current legal review.
- Thailand's official PDPA materials and CAAT passenger-rights/fare guidance require local verification for privacy, price display and disruption communications.

## Recommendation

Adopt VJ-GMOM with:

1. Four North-Star layers: incremental contribution, customer value, future demand and operating trust.
2. A closed operating loop: `SENSE → CHOOSE → DESIGN OFFER → CREATE → ACTIVATE → SERVICE → MEASURE → LEARN`.
3. A group hub, capability chapters and parameterized local market pods.
4. Six missing capabilities: brand/portfolio, CX, marketing science, content discovery, MarTech/AI Ops and market-pod leadership.
5. Typed work objects, dependency-aware routing, independent reviewers and human gates.
6. A maturity claim limited to Level 2 until real connectors, identity/permissions, production experiments and release controls are validated.
