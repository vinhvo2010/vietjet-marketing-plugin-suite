## Handoff rule

Any unapproved or missing route, date, fare, offer, inventory, eligibility, or terms item must be omitted, replaced with a bracketed placeholder, or handed to `vietjet-commercial-ops`. Creative work may continue using a non-commercial concept direction when useful.
